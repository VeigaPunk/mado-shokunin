## C1 — coverage: the margined auto exclusive-zone composition becomes a modelled join

**Claim.** `tools/mado-barfoot.py` (r78-v1) forms the reserved-band expectation for a margined
exclusive bar by composing the two measured contributors: gtk-layer-shell 0.10.1's auto zone
(the allocation plus the margin of each *unanchored* edge of the band axis) and Hyprland
v0.56.2 `applyExclusive` (the exclusive zone plus the anchored edge's margin). For a top or
bottom bar the band is `height + margin.top + margin.bottom`; transposed for a vertical bar it
is `width + margin.left + margin.right`; a negative margin is refused, never composed. The
frozen case set also exercises the three gaps the round-77 mutation tester named: a
right-anchored bar, a `layer: bottom` bar, and an exclusion-only `output` spec.
