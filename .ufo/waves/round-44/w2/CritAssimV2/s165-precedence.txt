== S165.bin ordering/precedence ==
63:Static effects are evaluated once when the window is opened and never again.
134:Dynamic effects are re-evaluated every time a property changes.
315:Effects marked as _dynamic_ are re-evaluated whenever the matching property of the window changes.
318:Effects are processed top to bottom, and the _last_ match takes precedence.
