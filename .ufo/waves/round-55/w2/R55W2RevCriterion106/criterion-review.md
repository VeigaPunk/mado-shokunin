## Style and non-duplication (criteria 102–105, 64)

- **Conflict: two launch records.** Criterion 102 (`corpus/QUALITY.md:569`) says "the committed `<round>/<wave>.ready` marker is the launch record". Criterion 106 (`:586`) says "the wave's launch instant is the `createdAt` of its committed `<wave>/swarm-registry.json`". The two can disagree (round 55: the `.ready` marker's own stamp is 62 min after `createdAt`, see above). The criteria need one definition, or 106 must say which record is the launch for the freeze comparison and why the other is not.
- **Receipt not named.** Criteria 102, 103 and 105 each name a committed receipt or fixture path for their shown-able-to-fail evidence (`receipts/criteria-red-green.txt:116-121`; `.ufo/waves/round-51/w2/…`). 106 quotes the output with no path. A committed receipt exists (`.ufo/waves/round-55/w1/R55ExecFreezeOrder/r54-run-output.txt`); 106 should cite it, as criterion 79 requires for a recorded run.
- **Overlap with 64, not duplication.** Criterion 64 (`:375`) checks that counter paths exist at "the freeze commit" before dispatch, and takes that commit as an input. 106 defines the freeze commit (earliest first-parent commit at which the set has its REV blobs). The two definitions agree in practice, but 64 does not cross-reference 106 and does not define the term. 106 should either say it supersedes 64's definition or point to it.
- **Non-duplication statement missing.** Criterion 102 says explicitly that it is "not a restatement of 99 or 87". 106 has no equivalent sentence; it is not a restatement of 102–105, but it should say so, since 102 also reads launch records.
- **Path form.** The header uses `lessons/round-54.md`; 102, 103 and 105 use `corpus/lessons/…`. Both forms occur in the file; the header's `lessons/round-55.md` does not resolve at HEAD (T2).
- **Wave scope.** 106 says "for each wave" but does not say how the per-wave checks are run or recorded. The lane has w1 and w2 (`receipts/r55-w2-at-721ba9c1.txt`: `verdict=OK`, rc 0, launch `23:19:14.796Z`, after the `5a43d67f` errata). There is no loop, no committed per-wave receipt for round 55, and the criterion names only w1.
