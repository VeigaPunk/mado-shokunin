## Proposed QUALITY criterion for the largest uncaught gate-delivery gap

Every runnable quality gate or probe relied on for a round’s verdict must have a SKILL.md registry row before it is relied on, naming its purpose, exact entry command, accepted input path and format, verdict and exit-code map, self-test, pinned implementation, write scope, and owner. Its committed self-test must include a passing fixture in the round’s actual receipt format and a failing fixture for each registered rejection condition; a missing row or rejection of valid current-round receipts makes the gate nonconformant and unable to support a verdict.
