  reading: BOTH harnesses copy the outside bytes (nfiles=1, root_canary_copied=true, sha256 matches
  the outside file). snapshot() resolves each of the four roots with os.path.realpath(src) before
  os.walk, and the round-27 guard (oracle.py:78) tests only symlinked *files* met during the walk,
  so an outside-HOME *root* symlink is followed by both harnesses. The r27 fix is scoped to
  file-level symlinks; a root symlink is not covered. Consequence for callers that snapshot a home
  whose one of the four roots is such a symlink: outside bytes enter the snapshot (and, for C1, any
  tree copied from it) under either harness.
