=== 2 plug, unplug all, plug again
  plug [Foo] -> [foo]   live: foo
  unplug [foo]   live:
  plug [Foo] -> [foo]   live: foo
=== 3 physical device already named -1 plugs first
  plug [Foo 1] -> [foo-1]   live: foo-1
  plug [Foo] -> [foo]   live: foo-1 foo
  plug [Foo] -> [foo-2]   live: foo-1 foo foo-2
=== 4 duplicate pair then a device whose name maps to foo-1
  plug [Foo] -> [foo]   live: foo

=== 5 first of a duplicate pair unplugged, a new identical unit plugged
  plug [Foo] -> [foo]   live: foo

  plug [Foo] -> [foo]   live: foo-1 foo

=== 7 keyboard and mouse share the namespace
