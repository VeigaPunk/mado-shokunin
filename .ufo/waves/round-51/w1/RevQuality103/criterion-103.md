103. A round report may cite a WAVE-DONE or settled verdict only when a committed receipt for that exact verdict exists in the round's wave directory at the report's integration revision. The receipt must identify the checked revision and round, and its recorded outcome must match the report's claim; a pre-integration receipt, a receipt for an earlier partial wave set, or an uncommitted rerun does not support a final closure assertion. Check: rerun the round's `quality/check-wave-done.py` (or the registered equivalent) against the report's integration revision, capture its complete output and exit status in a receipt under `.ufo/waves/round-N/`, commit that receipt with the report, and verify both paths resolve at the same revision. Declared limit: a receipt proves only the checker output and revision it records; it does not prove the checker is sound, that every report assertion cites the receipt, or that the report was committed after the check unless the integration process preserves and verifies that ordering. The checker's own wave-discovery and settled semantics remain governed by its contract (including committed `.ready` scope).
