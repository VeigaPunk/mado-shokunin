## 4. What the measurements constrain (non-binding; no predicate amended)

### C1 (operability)

1. **Production can satisfy "non-self", not "unique target".** 0/82 self-pointers, 12 distinct pointer
   files, 62/82 rows on `SCORECARD.md`+`PROVINCE.md` (§2.1c). A counter clause demanding
   per-instrument evidence files cannot return TARGET_MET on this ledger; only the r51 shape
   (non-self + resolvable + basename-bearing) is currently true of production.
2. **Description discrimination is the whole open surface.** Both existing instruments reject only
   blanks/placeholders (§2.1b). Any clause that binds a description to its instrument must be tested
   against the ledger at base *before* it is frozen, because 82 rows exist and a strict clause is a
   TARGET_MISSED risk on production — the same tension the r52 draft died on (forged description
   passing a lexical context test, `corpus/lessons/round-52.md` M52-1).
3. **The counter's own row is constitutive.** Adding a file under `quality/counters/` raises `P`
   from 82 to 83 (§2.1a), and the r51 coverage/uniqueness clauses then require a new ledger row for
   it; a new counter that does not update `quality/tool-ledger.md` fails its own coverage clause on
   the production arm it must pass.
4. **Control arm needs a frozen fixture, not a prose control.** The r53 target names "a no-work
   control and each frozen forgery fixture"; what exists to freeze today is
   `.ufo/waves/round-52/controls/c1-do-nothing.md`, `Falsify43/control-v2-fixture-ledger.md` (repeated
   generic self-pointers) and the mechanically generated `Falsify43/bypass-ledger.md` — each with a
   committed receipt for the *r52 draft* hardware, not for an r53 counter.

### C2 (coverage)

1. **A contiguity/word-count counter catches only P16.** My mechanical census shows 17/18 quotes
   contiguous and inside their anchors (§2.2b); P17's and P18's quotes are verbatim and correctly
   anchored — their defects are in the proposition text (subject, localization). A "frozen counter
   rejects the old bytes" clause therefore needs either a binding the proposition→pin relation
   actually tests, or the target's own second leg (independent reviewer) does that work. Stating it
   plainly: mechanical quote binding, as implemented in any counter seen so far, cannot reject P17/P18.
2. **P16 cannot be repaired by widening the quote.** `:1554`→`:1576` is 23 source lines and 69
   whitespace tokens `[OBS]`; ≤25 words forbids a single contiguous excerpt covering both calls. The
   two clean repairs are (a) split into
   two rows (`:1554` mods-before, 4 words; `:1576` mods-cleared, 4 words) or (b) keep one row and mark
   the clear clause as located-not-quoted. `[OBS]` on the word counts of both candidate quotes.
3. **P17 must be merged or re-scoped, not re-labelled.** Its anchor is P04's anchor, both quotes are
   subsets of the same `:619-622` fact; a corrected entry that keeps both is either a duplicate
   (inflating "18 propositions") or a second proposition that must say something P04 does not.
4. **P18's repair is a wording deletion** ("inside the scan" → after the loop, in the cache-miss
   branch) with the `:573-574` bytes as the ground.
5. **The index defect is part of the coverage repair.** §2.2e: fixing P16–P18 without moving the row
   into the coverage-map table leaves `check-index-rows.py` red and the entry outside the map the
