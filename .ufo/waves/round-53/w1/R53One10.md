   of §3.2 is the minimal discriminating design found: dropping `e-subject`/`e-locus` lets

   family vocabulary; the subject rule only fires for backticked identifiers that are function

   ids live under `/fast/scratch/hyaku/mado-r53/R53One10/` and were removed on exit; every
   measurement above is reproducible from the recipes in §2.2, §3.3–3.5 and the appendices.
