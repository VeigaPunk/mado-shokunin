## Unsupported or materially qualified claims to correct before freeze

1. Step 12 “relaxed only by `i`” omits special-dispatcher `IGNORECONDITIONS` and `submapUniversal` bypasses (`KeybindManager.cpp:638-642,652`).
2. Step 14's “off-by-8 ... never fires” is false as an absolute; wrong code can match another event.
3. Step 16 first-config-order-wins contradicts accumulation/dispatch of all eligible matches (`KeybindManager.cpp:773-781`).
4. Step 17's release sentence reverses the press-time-mask check (`KeybindManager.cpp:736-745`); release semantics can make it fail after modifier changes.
5. Step 17's “release/nonConsuming/autoConsuming only decide swallowing” is overbroad; they affect which events queue and `found` state (`:728-753,835-837`).
6. Step 11's universal Caps/NumLock impossibility is not demonstrated by the cited lines; distinguish the observed depressed-only mask from a universal lock-state invariant.
7. Step 7 makes reference-census/unreachable-loop assertions without sufficient locators; either add exact evidence or qualify them.
8. Entry does not explain `repeat` behavior; pinned `:827-833` schedules repeating binds.
