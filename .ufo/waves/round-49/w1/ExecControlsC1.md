  `score-with-draft.py` and require these four to stay TARGET_MISSED; a thinner file would bless them.

  real and is closed by g alone: `c17` matches all 633 fixtures byte for byte, keeps `selftest=ok`, `readonly=ok`,

  published before a tool is frozen, and `gen_case` must keep drawing keymaps outside the fixture file.
- **F4** The write and socket halves of clause r are load-bearing on their own: `c18`/`c19` pass all 633 fixtures,
  the selftest and the read-only test up to the trip (`trips=693` and `1386`), and only r stops them once g is off.
  Do not reduce r to "no execve".
- **F5** The refusal probes in clause s are load-bearing: `c20` answers the whole fixture file (f=633/633,
