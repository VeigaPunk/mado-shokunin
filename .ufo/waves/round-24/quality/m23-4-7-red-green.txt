== 7. What this receipt does not establish ==
- The check classifies populations by path class and registration manifest; it
  does not judge whether the fixtures are meaningful discovery attacks or
  whether a registered case was ever executed.
- Only committed history is visible: fixtures written to the work tree but not
  committed at the time of a tools/ change are invisible to the check.
- Commit dates are printed for context only; ordering comes from
  `git rev-list --reverse --topo-order`, so rebases and non-monotonic clocks
  affect the reported dates, not the verdict.
- A `repair/` population counts only when its manifest declares discovery or
  attack; this is the interpretation that makes the routed round-23 defect
  (M23-4, M23-7) visible. Round-23 repair/expected.json (schema
  mado-r23-repair-set-v1) names neither word.
- It is a registration-order check, not a judgement that the registered
  population covers the defect the tools/ change addresses.
