# clause attribution — every frozen reject control kills its own named clause

The frozen manifest's ten inherited round-35 c1 crib trees carry the registry table
WITHOUT a `## Registry` heading, so under PRED-C1-r36-v1 they die at the row-in-registry
fence before their named clause is reached. To prove each named clause is load-bearing on
its own, each tree was copied to /tmp with the heading restored and re-scored:

$ for id in c1-donothing c1-digest c1-entry-symlink c1-quote-absent c1-quote-long c1-recheck-prose c1-row-duplicate c1-row-extra-field c1-status-queued c1-usedby-extra; do cp -a .ufo/waves/round-35/controls/$id /tmp/w36-clauseattr/$id; (insert ## Registry heading); python3 -I quality/counters/c1-idle-lock.py --root /tmp/w36-clauseattr/$id --frozen .ufo/waves/round-36/c1/frozen.json; done
