## Gate (S072+ skeleton)

Rules for every new row (drafts write `S0xx-pending`; L1 assigns the ids in `corpus/SOURCES.md`):

- URL: a tag-pinned raw URL where one exists (`raw.githubusercontent.com/<owner>/<repo>/<tag>/<path>`). Record the tag-to-commit line from `git ls-remote https://github.com/<owner>/<repo> refs/tags/<tag> 'refs/tags/<tag>^{}'`.
- Access date: the UTC date of `fetched_at` in the receipt line, not the date of drafting.
- Located quote: at most 25 words, verbatim, with a body `file:line` anchor. Count the words before filing.
- sha256: the digest of the saved BODY (not of the quote), as in the receipt line.
- Receipt: the path `.ufo/waves/round-18/fetch/<Seat>/receipts.jsonl` and the line number of the matching receipt.
- A row with no receipt is not credited. A tag's source does not prove the host binary.
