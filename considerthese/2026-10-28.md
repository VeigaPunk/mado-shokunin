## Top 3 lessons / gains

### 1. The desktop toolkit nearly doubled, and each new tool was checked on João's live host (rounds 44–50)

`mado-monitors.py` (r45), `mado-devices.py` (r46), `mado-terminal.py` (r47), `mado-keysym.py` (r49) and `mado-hostguard.py` (r50) join the four live-state tools from the last reflection. Each answers one practical question: does the monitor rule match what the compositor runs, is every input-device block still attached to a real device, which terminal will `xdg-terminal-exec` actually launch and why, and so on. The closures re-ran monitors and devices on the host and confirmed the receipts, and also showed the "can it fail" case (a scratch edit gives `DIFFER` / `STALE_BLOCK`). `mado-terminal.py` is judged against the real upstream script rather than a hand-written oracle, which is the strongest grounding the lane has used for a tool. This is the main capability gain in the window.

### 2. A test mutant touched João's real home directory, and the lane built a guard for it (rounds 48–50)

In round 48 an unsandboxed wave-2 mutant wrote to João's real `~/.cache/xdg-terminal-exec` (M48-10). It was a cache, it was rewritten by the next resolver run, and the lane said so openly. Round 49 then logged a digest of operator-owned state before and after the round, and round 50 landed `tools/mado-hostguard.py`. It runs a command under a scratch HOME and XDG dirs, or masks the home in bubblewrap, and fails if any declared operator path changed. It also refuses the real home as a work dir, with no override flag. The lesson: once the lane tests things against the live desktop, João's machine is inside the blast radius, so isolation has to be a tool, not a convention.

### 3. Counters that pass are not evidence by themselves, and the lane now stops instead of claiming (rounds 50–54)

Five rounds in a row, the falsification waves broke the lane's own admission counters. In r51 the ledger check passed with generic descriptions and self-pointers. In r52 forged quote cells passed. In r54 the quote judge gave 1/4 or 4/4 depending on whether whitespace is normalized. Instead of shipping a TARGET_MET, the lane recorded rounds 50, 52, 53 and 54 as honest stops. It also corrected itself both ways: r51's errata narrowed source-truth overclaims, and M51-3 withdrew a counterexample that turned out not to reproduce. The cost is that domain coverage stalled: knowledge stayed at 69 entries from round 53 on, and round 54 also broke its own rule that the freeze must be committed before wave 1. The lesson to carry: when the gate is the bottleneck, spend a round on the gate (frozen controls, exact fixtures, a non-author sentence-to-quote review) before going back to new ground.

**No gain in the window on:** non-Hyprland compositors, SHONEN promotion (still four historical `[~]`), PR #1, or an independent non-author review of the r51–r53 corrected prose (LOG says it is still pending).
